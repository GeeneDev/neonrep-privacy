NeonRep: Gym Rest Timer – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

This policy applies to the NeonRep: Gym Rest Timer app for Android (the "App"), published on Google Play by its independent developer (the "Developer", "we").

NeonRep keeps your data on your phone, and nothing is ever sent to the developer. It has no account, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect, share or sell any personal data. No registration is required.

Data you enter

Exercise names, sets, reps, rest times, notes, history, groups and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never sent to us. They only leave your phone if you save a backup yourself or through Android's own backup (see "Backup and restore" below).

Exercise photos

You can optionally add a photo to an exercise, either by taking one with your camera app or by choosing one from your gallery through Android's photo picker. The app only receives the single photo you pick. It is copied into the app's private storage on your phone and is never sent to us. It is only included in a backup if you save one (see "Backup and restore" below).

Purchases

The optional one-time "full version" unlock is processed by Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. The app only receives a confirmation from Google Play that the unlock was purchased, so it can enable the full version.

Notifications, alarms and the timer service

  • Notifications show the running rest timer and tell you when a rest is over.
  • Exact alarms make sure the "rest over" alert arrives on time even when the phone is idle. If you decline this permission, the app uses a less precise alarm.
  • A foreground service keeps the timer running while the app is in the background.

None of these features send any data off your device.

Location

The App does not access or collect your location.

Third parties

Because the App collects no data, we share nothing with third parties. The only outside service the App itself uses is Google Play, for installing the App and processing the optional purchase described above. If you choose to save a backup to a service such as Google Drive, that service stores the file for you (see below).

Backup and restore

You can save a backup file (your history, groups, rest times and exercise photos) to a location you choose, such as Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or a folder on your phone, and restore it later. The file is created and sent only when you ask, it goes only where you choose, and the developer never receives it. The chosen service's own privacy policy applies to files stored there.

The backup file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private. Restoring a backup replaces the data currently in the App.

Android may also back up the same app data to your Google account automatically, as part of the phone's own backup. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

You can delete history entries and photos inside the app. To remove everything, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Backup files you saved are not removed by this: delete them where you stored them.

If you email us, we only use your message and email address to reply. You can ask us at any time to delete that correspondence, and we will.

Security

Your data is kept in the App's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. A backup file you save is protected by wherever you store it. Since we hold none of your data, there is nothing on our side that could be exposed in a breach. If we ever hold data you sent us by email and it is affected by a breach, we will notify you as required by law.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Previous versions are available on request.
